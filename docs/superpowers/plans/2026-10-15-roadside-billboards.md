# Roadside advertising

Four two-sided static billboards at road-distance milestones 0, 100, 200 and 300 m on the existing 368 m rectangular road loop. The closing segment is 68 m; no road or lot is relocated. World scale follows the architectural plan (one unit per metre). Posts sit on the outer verge, away from the driving surface and pedestrian paths. The ground margin is extended to support them.

Each sign advertises R$ 35 per day. Signs share geometry/materials and a single canvas texture. Clicking a sign while on foot opens its enquiry panel; the neighborhood selector also exposes all signs for keyboard/mobile access. Contact uses the existing public email address, prefilled with sign ID, price and requested dates. No payment, reservation or availability claim is implemented.

Validation: distance/placement and enquiry tests, TypeScript/full build, browser enquiry and buggy regression; inspect neighborhood screenshot. Deployment is separate from this commit.
